# Suez University Faculty of Medicine: bylaw-based curriculum crosswalk

## Institution identity

This profile targets **Suez University (جامعة السويس), Faculty of Medicine / Faculty of Human Medicine (كلية الطب)**. It explicitly excludes **Suez Canal University (جامعة قناة السويس)**; that is a different institution and its curriculum must not be substituted.

## Source used

The primary source is the user-uploaded 44-page Arabic/English internal bylaw titled *Integrated Medicine and Surgery Programme, Faculty of Medicine – Suez University* (uploaded PDF, cited in this conversation as `turn407file0`). The bylaw is the basis for the programme structure, module names/codes, teaching approach, competency framework, assessment and progression summary below.

## Programme structure in the uploaded bylaw

- Five academic years.
- **Phase I – The Basics:** years 1 and 2, semesters 1–4.
- **Phase II – Clinical phase:** years 3, 4 and 5, semesters 5–10.
- The bylaw describes an ECTS credit framework.
- **Internship:** this uploaded bylaw does not specify internship duration, rotations or sign-off requirements. Do not assume a 2-year internship from this document alone; confirm separately with the current faculty regulations.

## Topic-to-module crosswalk

The machine-readable file assigns the existing 62 Usiphu topics to the explicit bylaw module(s) where the specialty/system is named. Examples:

- Urology topics → Urinary System (US 1.2.9) and Surgery I (SI 2.4.2); the bylaw explicitly lists urology within Surgery I.
- General surgery topics → Surgery I (SI 2.4.2).
- Paediatrics → PHC/ped 2.3.1.
- Obstetrics and gynaecology → Reproductive system (RS 1.2.11) and PHC/Obs 2.3.2.
- Internal medicine topics → the matching early system module where applicable (cardiovascular CS1.1.4; respiratory RS 1.1.3; gastrointestinal GS 1.2.6; endocrine EM 1.2.10; urinary US 1.2.9) plus Medicine I (MI 2.4.1).
- Neurology topics → CNS (NS 1.2.7) and Medicine II (MII 2.5.1); neurosurgical topics also link to Surgery I (SI 2.4.2).
- Dermatology and psychiatry → Medicine II (MII 2.5.1).
- ENT, ophthalmology and orthopaedics → Special Senses (SS 1.2.8) or Musculoskeletal System (MS 1.1.2), respectively, plus Surgery II (SII 2.5.2).
- Vascular surgery is treated as a related department alignment under Surgery I/general surgery, not as a separately coded block.
- Anaesthesia/critical-care topics are **not** assigned an invented standalone module: the department appears in the faculty department list, but the course table does not give it a separate code.

These are **module-level associations**, not proof that every individual learning objective is an official module outcome.

## Teaching and learning model

The bylaw describes community-oriented/community-based learning, problem-based learning in small groups, integration of basic and clinical sciences, student-directed learning, comprehensive assessment and evidence-based medicine. It also describes early clinical skills and primary-care exposure.

## Competency framework

The bylaw defines six graduate competency areas:
1. Graduate as a healthcare provider.
2. Graduate as a healthcare developer and advocate.
3. Basic medical knowledge and information.
4. Professional ethics and professionalism.
5. Member of the healthcare team and health system.
6. Evidence-based practice and lifelong learning.

## Assessment and progression (summary)

- Weekly formative feedback is described as non-graded.
- Summative assessment is divided into periodic assessment (75%) and end-of-phase assessment (25%).
- Periodic assessment includes a continuing portfolio and end-of-semester/group assessment.
- Phase I uses integrated practical/OSPE and written assessment; Phase II uses clinical stations/OSCE or laboratory assessment and written MCQs.
- The final Phase II assessment includes written clinical problem-solving/competency components and clinical stations.
- The bylaw states a minimum attendance requirement of 75% per educational group.
- Progression depends on passing the required educational groups/units; consult the bylaw's detailed progression clauses before encoding individual edge cases.

## Important limitations

1. The uploaded bylaw gives module titles/codes and broad specialty groupings, but not the detailed course-specification learning outcomes for every module.
2. It does not specify internship duration or rotations.
3. Some departments listed by the faculty are not separately coded in the five-year module table.
4. The uploaded copy's current applicability to a particular student cohort has not been independently confirmed.
5. Automated validation cannot establish faculty approval or exam validity.
