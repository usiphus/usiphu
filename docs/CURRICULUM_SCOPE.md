## What these numbers mean

- **Mapped** means a topic exists in the blueprint with at least one learning objective.
- **Sourced** means a topic has explicit references to current authoritative sources.
- **Reviewed** means a qualified subject-matter educator has reviewed the topic and its objectives.
- **Exam validated** means the topic has been mapped to the selected examination blueprint and reviewed against representative assessment objectives.

At this snapshot, all 62 topics are mapped; **0 are marked sourced, 0 clinically reviewed, and 0 exam validated**. The starter MCQ and case items are explicitly marked `draft`. These counts are shown in the app and API so topic volume is not mistaken for verified curriculum completeness.

## Required decision for a complete syllabus

The project does not yet name a target syllabus or jurisdiction. A "complete" curriculum cannot be asserted until the target is selected. The target could be an Egyptian medical school / postgraduate board syllabus, a named college or specialty board, or an international framework. Once selected, record its title, version, issuing body, and date; map every syllabus objective to one or more Usiphu topics; then record source and reviewer metadata.

## Completion workflow

For each specialty and topic:

1. **Mapped** — topic and measurable learning objectives exist.
2. **Sourced** — add stable guideline, textbook, journal, or open resource references.
3. **Reviewed** — record reviewer identity/role and review date.
4. **Exam validated** — link the target syllabus objective and confirm assessment coverage.
5. **Maintained** — record next review date and retire superseded recommendations.

Do not use the topic count or the percentage of topics with objectives as a proxy for syllabus completion. Management recommendations must carry jurisdiction and guideline edition where relevant.
