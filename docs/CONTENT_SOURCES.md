# Resource discovery and content provenance

Usiphu links learners to external discovery services; it does not scrape or redistribute decks, articles, question banks, or publisher content.

## General discovery sources

| Source | Intended use | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| PubMed | Biomedical citations and abstracts | A citation index, not a full-text repository. |
| PubMed Central | Open full-text biomedical articles | Reuse rights vary by article; inspect the license. |
| NCBI Bookshelf | Books, reports, and reviews | Check edition and update date. |
| Cureus | Open-access medical journal articles | Read critically; check methods, conflicts, and article-specific reuse terms. |
| AnkiWeb | User-shared flashcard decks | User-contributed; verify accuracy and deck licensing before importing or redistributing. |
| DoctorDE | Medical challenge discovery | Current integration is a web-search link only; a stable official endpoint has not been verified. |
| WHO / NICE | Public health and clinical guidance | Jurisdiction and local protocols may differ. |
| Cochrane | Systematic reviews | Check currency and certainty of evidence. |
| MedEdPORTAL | Peer-reviewed teaching resources | Follow resource-specific permissions. |

## Specialty guideline hubs

The resource finder appends a relevant guideline hub for recognized specialty labels, including EAU (urology), ESC (cardiology), KDIGO (nephrology), GINA (asthma), GOLD (COPD), IDSA (infectious disease), ACOG (obstetrics), AAP (paediatrics), AAO (ophthalmology), SVS (vascular surgery), ASA (anaesthesia), AAN (neurology), AAD (dermatology), and APA (psychiatry).

These are starting points, not automatic endorsements. Prefer the latest guideline from the responsible professional society or national authority and check whether it applies to the learner's country and patient population.

## Content provenance fields required before publication

Each locally authored learning item should carry:
- Stable item ID and specialty/topic mapping.
- Source title, stable URL or DOI, publisher/issuing body, and publication/update date.
- Access classification (open full text, abstract only, subscription, or community-contributed).
- Copyright/license note where reuse is contemplated.
- Author, clinical reviewer, review date, and next review date.
- Jurisdiction and guideline edition for management recommendations.
- Status: draft, clinically reviewed, exam-validated, or retired.

Do not convert a search result into an answer key without editorial review. Search ranking is not evidence quality. Do not import third-party Anki decks or copy article content unless the license and terms permit the intended use.
